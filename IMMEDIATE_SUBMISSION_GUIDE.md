# 🚀 Strago App - Immediate Submission Guide

## READY TO SUBMIT NOW!

Your Strago app is ready for App Store submission. Here's exactly what to do:

### Step 1: Wait for Build (5-10 minutes)
The current build with correct bundle ID `com.strago.app` should complete soon.

### Step 2: Go to App Store Connect (2 minutes)
1. Open: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/
2. Click "My Apps" → "+" → "New App"

### Step 3: Create App Listing (5 minutes)
Fill in these exact details:

**Basic Info:**
- **Platform**: iOS
- **Name**: Strago
- **Primary Language**: English (U.S.)
- **Bundle ID**: com.strago.app (will appear after build completes)
- **SKU**: strago-ios-app

**App Information:**
- **Name**: Strago
- **Subtitle**: Scan - Track - Go
- **Category**: Shopping
- **Price**: Free

### Step 4: Add Description (2 minutes)
Copy and paste this description:

```
Strago helps you save money on groceries by tracking prices, managing budgets, and providing smart shopping insights.

TRACK YOUR SPENDING
• Create shopping sessions with spending limits
• Scan barcodes to add products instantly
• Monitor your cart in real-time
• Get alerts when approaching your budget

COMPARE PRICES
• Track prices across multiple stores
• See price trends over time
• Get notifications on price drops
• Find the best deals automatically

SHOPPING INSIGHTS
• View detailed analytics on your spending
• See which stores offer the best prices
• Identify your most-purchased items
• Get personalized recommendations

SMART FEATURES
• Barcode scanner for quick product entry
• Photo capture for product records
• Multi-store support
• Budget tracking per session
• Purchase history with receipts
• Analytics dashboard

Perfect for budget-conscious shoppers who want to make informed decisions and save money on every grocery trip!
```

**Keywords**: `grocery,shopping,price,tracker,budget,scanner,strago,barcode,savings,deals`

**Support URL**: https://strago.app/
**Privacy Policy URL**: https://strago.app/privacy

### Step 5: Upload Screenshots (10 minutes)

- iPhone 6.7" Display (1290 x 2796 pixels)
- iPhone 6.5" Display (1284 x 2778 pixels)

**Quick Screenshot Creation:**
1. Use iOS Simulator or your device

   - Shopping cart with items
   - Analytics dashboard

### Step 6: App Review Information (3 minutes)
Add these notes for Apple reviewers:

```
DEMO ACCOUNT:
Email: [Your demo account email]
Password: [Your demo account password]

TESTING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Log in with demo account
2. Tap "Start Grocery" to create new session
3. Enter store details and budget
4. Use Scan tab to add products
5. Grant camera permission when prompted
6. Scan barcode or enter manually: 123456789
7. Add product details and save
8. View cart and complete checkout
9. Check Analytics for insights

FEATURES TO TEST:
- Barcode scanning with camera
- Product price tracking
- Budget management
- Shopping history
- Analytics dashboard

PERMISSIONS:
- Camera: Required for barcode scanning
- Photo Library: Optional for product photos
- Location: Optional for store tracking

BACKEND: Supabase for authentication and database
NOTE: Internet connection required
```

### Step 7: Select Build & Submit (2 minutes)
1. Your build will automatically appear in "Build" section
2. Select the latest build with `com.strago.app`
3. Complete age rating (4+ - No objectionable content)
4. Click "Submit for Review"
