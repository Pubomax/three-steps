# Privacy Policy for Three Steps

**Last Updated: January 25, 2025**

## Introduction

Three Steps ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: Email address and password (encrypted)
- **Shopping Data**: Product names, brands, prices, quantities, store names, and locations
- **Product Photos**: Images you capture of products using your device camera
- **Session Data**: Shopping session names, dates, budgets, and shopping types

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the app, features used, and time spent
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Location Data**: Store locations when you enter them (not continuous tracking)

### Information from Third Parties

- We use Supabase for backend services, which may collect additional technical data for service provision

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- **Provide Core Services**: Track your shopping, manage your cart, and calculate budgets
- **Price Tracking**: Compare prices across stores and shopping trips
- **Analytics**: Show you spending insights and shopping patterns
- **Improve the App**: Understand how features are used and identify areas for improvement
- **Customer Support**: Respond to your requests and provide assistance
- **Security**: Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and fraud

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage Location
- All data is stored on secure Supabase servers (hosted on AWS)
- Images are stored in Supabase Storage with public URLs
- Database access is protected by Row-Level Security (RLS)

### Security Measures
- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
- Row-Level Security ensures users can only access their own data
- Regular security updates and monitoring

### Data Retention
- Your account data is retained as long as your account is active
- You can request account deletion at any time (see Your Rights section)
- Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do NOT:
- ❌ Sell your personal information to third parties
- ❌ Share your shopping data with retailers or brands
- ❌ Use your data for advertising or marketing by third parties
- ❌ Track your location continuously

### We MAY Share Data:
- **With Service Providers**: Supabase (database hosting), which has their own privacy policies
- **For Legal Compliance**: If required by law, court order, or government request
- **To Protect Rights**: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of users
- **With Your Consent**: When you explicitly authorize sharing

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control
- **View Your Data**: Access all your shopping data within the app
- **Edit Information**: Update product details, session names, and account information
- **Delete Data**: Remove individual shopping trips or products
- **Account Deletion**: Request full account deletion by emailing privacy@threesteps.app

### Permissions Control
- **Camera**: Required for barcode scanning and product photos. Can be revoked in device settings.
- **Location**: Optional for store location tracking. Can be disabled in device settings.

### Marketing Communications
- We currently do not send marketing emails
- Future newsletters will include an unsubscribe option

## Children's Privacy

Three Steps is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws different from your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Sending an email notification (for significant changes)

Your continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
- Right to say no to the sale of personal information
- Right to access your personal information
- Right to equal service and price

**We do not sell your personal information.**

## European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under GDPR:
- **Right to Access**: Request copies of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your data
- **Right to Restrict Processing**: Request limitation on data use
- **Right to Data Portability**: Request transfer of your data
- **Right to Object**: Object to processing of your data
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent at any time

**Legal Basis for Processing**:
- **Contract Performance**: To provide the service you signed up for
- **Legitimate Interests**: To improve our service and prevent fraud
- **Consent**: Where you've given explicit permission
